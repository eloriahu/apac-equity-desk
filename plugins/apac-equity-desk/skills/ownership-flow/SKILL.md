# Ownership and Flow

Before choosing tools, apply `../../references/tool-selection.md`, including
when this skill is invoked directly. Reuse the lead’s plan in supporting work.

Use the `ownership-analysis` module in
`../../references/research-modules.md`: read and execute the installed
`fundamental-tools:ownership-flow-review` skill for the scoped evidence/calculation
assignment when available. Reuse its returned pack; a missing skill or runtime
uses the documented fallback with the same evidence requirements.

Resolve listing and date window, then seek official shareholder disclosures or entitled market-flow data. AI Toolbox can supply `ownership_flow/v1`, including optional FinMind Taiwan institutional-share flows and pykrx Korea investor net KRW trading value; other markets may need user-supplied or official data. Do not imply that optional tools, tokens or entitlements are present. Follow `../../references/research-contracts.md` and source-priority rules.

Report flow category, unit, period, update lag, revisions, ownership type and the filing date. Do not mix KRW value with share volumes or treat a nominee/custodian/category as a beneficial owner. Test whether the flow is exceptional versus a properly sourced baseline before mentioning positioning. A flow can be consistent with a move but cannot alone establish its cause. Run verification and editing, state missing coverage, and retain `DRAFT — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED`.
