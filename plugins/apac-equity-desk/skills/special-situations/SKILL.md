# Special Situations

Read [research standards](../../references/event-driven/research-standards.md). Establish scope,
cutoff and universe actually searched. A web shortlist is not a complete database.
Separate announced transactions, preliminary approaches and rumors.

Require a discrete event, value mechanism, holder eligibility, timeframe, market
expectation, testable mispricing, downside and falsifier. Cheap valuation alone is
not an event. Use event-specific questions:

| Event | Economics to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Takeover / privatization | Terms, conditions, hedge, standalone downside; use merger-arb |
| Spin-off | Entitlement, parent ex-value, debt/cost allocation, when-issued trading, evidence of forced selling |
| Tender / buyback | Eligibility, deadlines, proration, residual price, odd-lot exclusions; do not assume full acceptance |
| Rights / recapitalization | Subscription price, ratio, dilution, rights value/transferability, cash required and lapse risk |
| Activism / strategic review | Actual filing, voting power, board response, credible mechanism and dated catalyst |
| Asset sale / liquidation | Net proceeds after liabilities/tax/leakage, waterfall, trapped cash and distribution dates |
| Distressed equity | Claim priority, dilution, financing, court and recovery waterfall; enterprise recovery is not equity recovery |
| Index / forced flow | Official rules/announcement, effective date, flow assumptions and liquidity; anticipated inclusion is not confirmed |

Rank by evidence and eligibility, then net scenario economics, timing, liquidity,
borrow, downside and common risk. Avoid unexplained scores, generic historic-return
promises or ranking solely by annualized spread. Return material rejections; zero
qualifying candidates is valid. Attach source/time and missing inputs per candidate.

For portfolio research, identify common regulators, buyers, lenders, sectors,
countries and close windows. Stress simultaneous breaks, delays, tighter borrow
and gaps. Dollar-neutral stock deals can retain event, factor and liquidity risk.
Use a research watchlist until capital and portfolio limits are supplied.

For strategy development, define eligible universe, point-in-time event timestamps,
entry/exit rules, costs, risk limits and benchmark before assessing performance.
Include failed and withdrawn deals; check look-ahead, survivorship, stale prices,
borrow and corporate actions. No backtest claims without reproducible data and
calculations. Use an available quant skill only when the requested work needs it.
