# Jurisdiction sources

Determine governing regimes from issuer, incorporation and transaction structure;
listing venue alone is insufficient. Retrieve current official rules and deal
documents each time. This is a discovery map, not legal thresholds.

| Market | Official starting points / questions |
| --- | --- |
| Hong Kong | [SFC takeovers](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Corporates/Takeovers-and-mergers), [codes](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes), [practice notes](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Corporates/Takeovers-and-mergers/Practice-notes), HKEXnews. Code applicability, scheme/offer structure, holder classes, court steps. |
| Japan | EDINET, TDnet, issuer tender documents, JFTC. Tender limits/conditions, competing bids, squeeze-out and classes. |
| Australia | ASX, ASIC, Takeovers Panel, ACCC, FIRB, court and scheme documents. Distinct vote/court, competition and investment steps. |
| Singapore | SGXNet, MAS/Securities Industry Council, CCCS, court and offer/scheme documents. |
| Mainland China | SSE/SZSE/BSE, CSRC, SAMR; share classes, approvals, payment and cross-border constraints. |
| Korea / Taiwan / India | DART/KRX and KFTC; MOPS/TWSE and TFTC; NSE/BSE, SEBI and CCI; transaction documents. |
| US | SEC EDGAR agreements, proxies, S-4/tender filings/amendments; [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program), DOJ, courts, Treasury CFIUS when relevant. Waiting-period status is not every approval. |
| UK / EU | Takeover Panel, issuer scheme/offer documents, CMA and relevant national-security review; European Commission and national authorities. |

Checked 2026-09-24: SFC landing/codes/practice-note pages and FTC program page.
Other entries are starting points to verify during research. The
[SEC tender glossary](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/tender-offer)
provides a general definition; deal-specific eligibility/proration require actual terms.
