# House style and templates

Write for a time-poor institutional reader. Lead with the move and the best-supported explanation. Use short paragraphs, active verbs and exact comparisons. Prefer three useful facts to ten headlines.

## Language rules

- `fell 4.1%, underperforming the battery basket by 2.3ppt` is better than `was weak`.
- Say `appears consistent with`, `likely contributed` or `may reflect` when causation is not confirmed.
- Reserve `on`, `after` and `driven by` for evidence whose timing and specificity support the link.
- Separate structural context from today's trigger. Label already-known issues as background.
- Attribute estimates and chatter inline. Preserve source links on the relevant claim.
- Avoid `obviously`, `clearly`, `massive`, `significant` and other unquantified intensifiers.
- Use local currency symbols/codes and a consistent sign convention.
- Do not hide missing data. Write `flow data unavailable as of 16:10 HKT`.

## Market-colour template

```text
DRAFT — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED

<NAME> <DESK TICKER> <MOVE>; <one-line relative observation>.

What/relative: <price, volume/turnover, peer/index spread, session/as-of>.
Catalyst: <highest-ranked confirmed driver, or state that no single catalyst is confirmed>.
Secondary factors: <ranked factors, with structural background kept separate>.
Flow/technical: <VWAP, turnover ratio, level or index contribution when supported>.
Chatter: <only if material; explicitly unconfirmed and attributed>.
Read-through: <A/H/ADR, peers, suppliers, customers, commodity or FX>.
Watch: <next confirmation or invalidation signal>.
Sources: <inline URLs and timestamps>.
```

Aim for 90–180 words for an intraday flash and up to 300 for a developed note.

## Market-wrap template

```text
DRAFT — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED

APAC close — <date>
Lead: <two sentences on the dominant regional regime and dispersion>.

China/Hong Kong — <indices>; <breadth>; <leading/lagging sectors>; <key movers/drivers>; <flows>.
Japan — ...
Korea — ...
Australia — ...
Singapore — ...

Cross-asset: <FX, rates and commodities that mattered, not a tape dump>.
Tomorrow: <3–5 scheduled catalysts and live questions>.
Data gaps: <material missing or stale inputs>.
```

Write market sections only for sessions with data. Target 450–800 words for the full regional wrap; shorten aggressively on low-information days.

## Trade-idea wording

Ideas are research scenarios, not instructions. Always state horizon, catalyst, expected transmission, confirmation, invalidation and key risks. Do not include order-entry language or personalized sizing.
