# Pre-open news snippets

This format follows the user's China news-digest example. The example supplies
editorial preferences only; its events, names, figures and dates are not a live
news source and must not be copied into fixtures or future notes.

## The writing job

Give an institutional reader a useful news scan before the selected market
opens. Each item answers what happened, who said or disclosed it, and which
details matter. A digest can contain many short items; "snippet" describes the
individual entry rather than a 150–250-word limit for the entire output.

Use this format for `China news snippets`, `HK pre-open snippets`, `before-market
news roundup` and equivalent requests in an APAC desk context. `Japan morning`
continues to mean a developed morning note. `Quick morning flash` continues to
mean a compressed morning view unless the user asks for a news digest.

## Presentation and voice

- Title the body `{Market} News Snippets:`. Put draft status and the date,
  cutoff/timezone and PRE-OPEN status in a compact wrapper. Mark historical
  preparation RETROSPECTIVE PRE-OPEN, with the requested information cutoff.
- Give each story its own paragraph, separated by a blank line. Normally use
  `Specific Headline: Factual summary` on the same line. Headlines identify the
  action or development; a colon separates a useful label from the news.
- Use one to three sentences for most items, often around 20–80 words. Give a
  complex facility, transaction or policy enough room to explain who qualifies,
  how it works and when it takes effect. Do not enforce a word quota.
- A brief company update may be a single company-led sentence without a separate
  headline. Put verified ticker(s) beside the company on first mention. Compact
  forms such as `603259.CH/2359.HK` are appropriate for this format when the
  company/listing mapping is verified; preserve leading zeroes. Never convert
  provider suffixes by guesswork. The usual `CH Equity` convention still applies
  to the other house formats.
- Preserve precise figures, currencies, periods and comparison bases. Prefer
  familiar compact units such as RMB6bn, USD900mn, YoY, QoQ and bps. Do not round
  away supplied precision. Keep quarterly results distinct from year-to-date
  totals and revised guidance distinct from the prior range.
- Attribute the claim directly: a company disclosed, a regulator proposed,
  a named outlet reported, or a broker forecast. A proposal is not an enacted
  rule and a reported test is not a product launch. Qualifications belong in
  the headline too, when omitting them would change the meaning.
- Use plain factual prose. Avoid routine "this highlights", "underscoring",
  "key takeaway", buy/sell language, invented colour and a summary conclusion.
  Do not add a market outlook, beneficiary list or watch section by default.
- Keep a concise source link beside each item, or closer to a particular claim
  if an item combines sources. Reader-facing copy should not expose source IDs,
  confidence scores or the research checklist. Put material coverage gaps after
  the digest in review notes.

## Selection and ordering

Scan domestic policy/macro, consequential global/geopolitical developments,
rates and market expectations, flows and industry developments, then company
announcements, results and guidance. For China, consider A-shares and relevant
Hong Kong listings, plus overseas developments with a credible China/HK channel.
These are coverage directions, not compulsory sections or a required roster.

Lead with the most consequential fresh stories. A useful default is major
global/domestic policy news, market and sector developments, then compact company
updates. Keep related items adjacent. Combine overlapping reports about the same
event when they add no distinct development; keep separate updates when their
timing or substance warrants it. No fixed top-five cutoff or target item count.
On a quiet day, return fewer items rather than stale news or filler.

Relevance guides selection; it does not require an appended trade implication
on every item. Include a short economic explanation when needed to understand
the news, such as how a liquidity facility addresses redemption pressure. Name
local beneficiaries only when the source/exposure supports them and they add
useful information. A company disclosure can stand as a factual update without
a stock move, peer basket, counterargument and falsifier attached.

## Source and timing checks

Research the interval from the previous local close to the requested cutoff,
including weekends and holidays. Older context belongs only where necessary to
understand a fresh development, with its date clear. Maintain event time,
publication/update time and quote time separately in the evidence record. Never
use news released after the cutoff in a retrospective pre-open digest.

Verify major policy changes, election outcomes, IPOs, large fundraisings and
unusual earnings figures against accessible primary evidence; use accurately
attributed reporting when confirmation is unavailable. Implausible scale calls
for a check of the original units, period and accounting measure, not a silent
correction. Do not turn an unsupported supplied headline into a polished fact.

For probability distributions, identify the observation time, meeting/horizon,
rate outcome and whether the move is cumulative. For flows, distinguish turnover
from net inflows and an observed buying burst from the buyer's identity or intent.
For reported price moves, identify the market, date and comparison basis. Check
any causal claim against the evidence; do not infer causation from adjacency.
An uncertain driver can be omitted or described briefly as unresolved without
turning the item into a full market-colour note.

## Synthetic layout illustration

The following is fictional formatting material, not market news:

```text
China News Snippets:

Example Regulator Proposes Settlement Change: The regulator opened consultation
on a shorter settlement cycle, with responses due on 12 May. The proposal does
not specify an implementation date.

Example Components Raises Revenue Guidance: Example Components lifted its
full-year revenue range to RMB4.2–4.4bn from RMB4.0–4.2bn, citing higher shipments.

Example Networks said its pilot line remains in qualification; commercial
deliveries are expected next year and no customer orders were disclosed.
```

Live output also needs the compact date/status wrapper and inline sources.
