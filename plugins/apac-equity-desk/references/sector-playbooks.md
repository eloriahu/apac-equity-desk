# APAC sector playbooks

These are investigation checklists, not standing causal claims. Verify every link for the current company, market and session.

| Sector | First observations | Candidate mechanisms to test | Frequent traps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semiconductors / hardware | foundry and memory peers, SOX handover, inventory, yields, export rules, FX | units, ASP, utilization, capex, customer mix | treating every AI headline as new; mixing designers, foundries and equipment |
| EV / batteries | OEM sales, battery prices, lithium/nickel, subsidies, tariffs, supplier disclosures | volume, content per vehicle, input cost, localization, utilization | using one OEM as the whole sector; confusing spot inputs with contracted cost |
| Internet / platforms | user and GMV indicators, ads, games approvals, regulation, take rates | engagement, monetization, margin, risk premium | stretching a macro headline across companies with different revenue models |
| Banks / insurance | curve, credit spreads, property, policy rates, capital, premium data | NIM, credit cost, investment yield, solvency, fee income | reading parallel moves as identical balance-sheet exposure |
| Autos | deliveries, incentives, model launches, dealer inventory, FX, tariffs | mix, pricing, utilization, export margin, warranty | comparing wholesale and retail data; ignoring launch timing |
| Consumer / tourism | traffic, bookings, ticket size, FX, weather, holidays, channel inventory | volume, mix, gross margin, operating leverage | non-comparable holiday calendars and base effects |
| Industrials | orders, PMI, capex, tender data, freight, metals, policy | backlog, utilization, input cost, working capital | treating policy intent as booked orders |
| Commodities / miners | commodity curve, grades, production, freight, FX, royalties | realized price, cost curve, volume, capex | applying spot moves one-for-one to earnings |
| Healthcare | trial/readout, approvals, reimbursement, product mix, licensing | probability-adjusted sales, milestones, margin, risk premium | treating early data as approval or class read-through as company fact |

For every sector ask: Why today? Is participation broad? Which names diverge? Is the news new? What contradicts the proposed mechanism? What dated fact would settle it?
