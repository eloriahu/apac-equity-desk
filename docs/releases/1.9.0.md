# APAC Equity Desk 1.9.0

Adds question-driven research planning above the tool-selection layer.

- The lead interprets the question, defines the necessary subquestions, selects scoped specialist assignments, sequences their inputs and revisits the plan after material findings.
- Ordinary earnings reviews explicitly invoke installed fundamental-tools:fundamental-research for normalized periods, operating drivers and earnings/cash quality, while retaining the desk's surprise/readiness helpers.
- Seven fundamental-tools specialists are mapped to financial analysis, expectations, filing changes, ownership, candidates, research audits and consensus challenges. Finance-tools, quant-tools and research-tools support market normalization, quantitative design and source investigation. Public Equity Investing retains ownership of requested full investment artifacts.
- Conditional assignments require their actual inputs. A single consensus snapshot cannot establish revisions; an EPS-only calculation or edit-only request stays narrow.
- Modules return evidence and calculations to one lead instead of starting competing reports, routers or agent teams. Source/claim identity, readiness and missing-input boundaries survive the handoff.
- The tool selector includes the specialist capabilities and unavailable-runtime/baseline cases. Semantic planning examples are acceptance criteria, not a measured model-accuracy result.

A local synthetic integration check executed the installed fundamental_pack.py helper and the desk earnings_surprise.py helper, preserving source IDs and identifying a cash-conversion follow-up without generating an unrequested DCF. This smoke check depends on the current host's optional installation; the core offline test suite has no cross-plugin runtime dependency.

No new connection, package installation, background job, trading action or publishing behavior is introduced. Existing Japan EOD presentation rules remain in place.
