# APAC Equity Desk 1.9.1

Makes multi-tool composition explicit and removes the fixed three-enricher cap.
One lead owns the final answer; a question may use as many relevant specialist
skills, tools and sequential follow-ups as its evidence needs require, within
explicit user scope, time, cost and access constraints.

Complementary roles are separate needs even when they share a capability. For
example, Docling extracts a table and an original PDF reader verifies its material
cells. The selector chooses a candidate per need, not a tool per question;
alternatives remain substitutes for that role. Existing agent concurrency limits
and source-independence rules are preserved.

A six-role earnings regression case covers extraction, original-page verification,
fundamental analysis, surprise calculations, estimate revisions and market reaction
under one lead. This tests plan composition, not live execution of all providers.
